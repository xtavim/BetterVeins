# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Break a whole deposit once the first chunk comes off, or on your first swing if you prefer
- All the ore drops in a neat stack at your feet instead of scattered across the ground
- No stutter, however big the deposit is
- Pickaxe wear and stamina still charged for every chunk
- Ore, scrap and rock can be turned on and off separately
- Hold a key to mine a single chunk
- Server settings sync with ServerSync
